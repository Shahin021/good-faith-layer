GOOD FAITH LAYER - STANDARD LIABILITY POLICY v1

Both parties agree, before the payment is made, that if these funds are later
reclassified as carrying upstream provenance risk, the loss will be allocated
according to this policy and not by default to whoever happens to be holding
the funds.

--- EVIDENCE ---

Two kinds of record are distinguished.

  Attested evidence is recorded by an attester registered with this contract,
  before acceptance. It is treated as evidence of fact.

  Recipient assertions are the recipient's own account of their conduct,
  submitted at acceptance. They describe what the recipient claims, not what
  happened. An assertion unsupported by attested evidence is at best unclear.

For a PROTECTED outcome, the attested record must also support the factual
findings that genuine value was exchanged and that no related-party indicators
were found, as well as showing that the required checks were performed. The
recipient's own assertions cannot establish those facts by themselves.

An attestation is fresh if it was recorded no more than the maximum
attestation age before acceptance. That maximum is stored on-chain as part of
this policy and is readable with get_max_attestation_age(policy_id). It is
deliberately not written here: a number in this text could drift from the
number the contract enforces, and the rule the parties read must be the rule
the contract runs.

Freshness is evaluated at the moment of acceptance and recorded then.

Assessment is made only on the record as it stood at acceptance. Nothing
submitted after the funds were flagged is considered.

--- REQUIRED CHECKS ---

  1. Screen the sending address against the provider risk list.
  2. Confirm the counterparty has prior settled history or verified identity.

A required check counts as performed when it was actually carried out, even
if its result was adverse or failed. The result is assessed separately,
including as notice where it creates a visible warning or red flag.

--- OUTCOMES ---

Outcome precedence is intentional. If related-party indicators are present,
REVIEW_REQUIRED takes precedence even when a no-value finding or a visible
warning would otherwise produce RECIPIENT_BEARS.

A missing or stale attestation blocks a PROTECTED outcome, but it does not
erase an independently established adverse finding. A finding that no genuine
value was exchanged, or that a visible warning existed at or before acceptance,
may still result in RECIPIENT_BEARS even when no attestation was recorded or
the attestation was stale.
PROTECTED. All of the following hold, on the record at acceptance:

  1. Genuine value was exchanged, consistent with the agreed terms, shown by
     attested evidence.
  2. No visible warning, adverse claim or red flag about these funds was
     available to the recipient at or before acceptance.
  3. The required checks above were carried out, shown by attested evidence.
  4. No related-party indicators are present, shown by attested evidence.
  5. An attestation exists and was fresh at acceptance.

  Conditions 1, 3 and 4 are affirmative findings and each must rest on attested
  evidence. The recipient's own account of the delivery, or of their
  relationship to the payer, does not establish them. Condition 2 is the
  reverse: a warning counts against the recipient wherever it appears, so it
  needs no attested support.

  Compensation is drawn in this order: first the payer's bond, then the
  protection pool, then the platform bond. The payer introduced the funds, so
  they carry the first loss before the mutualised pool is touched.

  If the reserves cannot cover the full amount, nothing is paid. The claim is
  not settled at a partial amount; it remains open and may be brought again
  once the reserves are funded.

RECIPIENT_BEARS. Either no genuine value was exchanged, or a visible warning
existed at or before acceptance. The loss stays with the recipient and nothing
is allocated.

REVIEW_REQUIRED. Everything else. No loss is allocated automatically and the
matter escalates to human review. In particular:

  - Related-party indicators are present. Where payer and recipient may be
    connected, no automatic payment is made to the recipient.
  - No attestation was recorded, or the attestation was stale at acceptance.
  - The evidence is insufficient to establish any of the PROTECTED conditions.
  - The assessment could not be produced in the required form, or the record
    contained text addressed to the assessor.

Ambiguity does not resolve in either party's favour.

--- SCOPE ---

This policy allocates an economic loss between the parties. It does not change
the legal status of any asset, does not clean funds, and does not lift any
restriction placed on them by a third party.
